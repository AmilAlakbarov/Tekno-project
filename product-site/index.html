01 — THE IDEA

Not just a label.
A cryptographic check.

Ordinary QR codes and printed serial numbers can be copied. AuthentiChain explores a stronger approach: an NFC tag generates changing, signed scan data that a server can validate against a registered product and secret key.

The result is designed to help a brand or operator distinguish a valid tag response from a replay, altered signature, unknown tag, or revoked identity.

05 — HONEST BY DESIGN

What a scan can—and can’t—tell you.

Cryptography can show that a tag produced valid scan data for a registered key. It cannot by itself prove who scanned it, where they are, or that the tagged item has not been physically moved or copied.

  • IP GeoIPApproximate network location; mobile networks, VPNs, and proxies can make it misleading.
  • Device GPSOptional and permission-based; may be denied, inaccurate, or spoofed.
  • Simulator / HSMSoftware demonstration components; not certified hardware security.
  • Fraud signalsUseful for human review; avoid blocking solely on a location estimate.
